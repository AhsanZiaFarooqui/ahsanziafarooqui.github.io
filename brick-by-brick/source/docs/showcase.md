# 3–5-minute showcase outline

Use actual evidence in the blanks. If it does not exist, say it has not yet been collected. Any prepared example learner must be labelled **synthetic demonstration**, kept outside real records, and never represented as a participant.

**0:00–0:35 — The question I investigated.** “I noticed __________. The evidence was __________. I chose reading time and fractions because __________.” State the hypothesis without claiming AI is better.

**0:35–1:15 — A fair comparison.** Show the two topic cards and describe eight initial questions, results, one of two tutorials, eight new comparable questions and final results. Explain practice choice versus random assignment. Explain why self-selected practice differences cannot show a causal effect.

**1:15–2:10 — Demonstrate the teaching.** Use a prepared, labelled synthetic practice session. Show a continuous clock hand or equal-sized fraction strips. Explain one actual design decision: __________. Both tutorial packages have the same controls, four segments and four guided opportunities. AI chooses checked wording/examples using initial evidence; it cannot change a quiz or draw arbitrary diagrams. If live AI is not verified, explicitly say this is a simulation.

**2:10–2:50 — Show honest scores.** Display a prepared synthetic before/after result, labelled synthetic. Explain “percentage points” and show that a result could improve, stay unchanged or decline. A single improvement does not establish an AI effect. Point out that incomplete quizzes remain missing.

**2:50–3:40 — Actual evidence and changes.** Number assigned and completing by group/topic: __________. Average gains and missing outcomes: __________. Failures: __________. Real anonymous feedback: __________. What I changed: __________. If no trial has run, state that clearly. Do not announce a winner from small, incomplete data. The dashboard’s complete-pair difference is not a full intention-to-treat estimate.

**3:40–4:30 — Ownership, limits and next steps.** “I decided __________; I received help from __________, who built __________; I checked __________. Next I would __________.” Explain that the forms need validation and the study compares the whole AI-tailored package, not the separate contribution of generative AI.

For a three-minute version, shorten the demonstration and feedback section. Keep the labels, ownership credit and limitations. Rehearse on the actual device; do not imply independent coding of features supplied by adults or Codex.
