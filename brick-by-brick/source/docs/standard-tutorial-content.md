## time-0: Two hands, one clock

The short thick hand tells hours. The long thin hand tells minutes. At o’clock, the long hand is at twelve. The short hand keeps moving between the numbers as minutes pass.

## time-2: Half past, quarter past, quarter to

Half of sixty is thirty: half past. A quarter is fifteen minutes. Quarter past looks back to the hour that started. Quarter to looks forward to the next hour.

Alternative: At one forty-five, forty-five minutes have passed and fifteen remain until two. That is quarter to two.
