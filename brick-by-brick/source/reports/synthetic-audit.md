Both policies share the first three broad questions. Fixed then uses the first focused item for each skill in curriculum order. Adaptive uses expected information gain, excluding seen families and limiting narrow probes. Stopping is deliberately disabled for both to hold the budget equal. A 0.5 classification threshold is used only for this synthetic metric, not to label children. Brier is the mean squared marginal error over five skills (lower better). Entropy is model uncertainty, not learning.

Matched responses use each item’s assumed slip and guess. The misspecified condition instead uses slip 0.22 and guess min(0.48, item guess + 0.12). No learning occurs in the simulation; no distractor generator or uncertainty/skip behaviour is invented. Synthetic truth is confined to the response generator and scoring. This modest audit covers five selected profiles rather than all children or all mastery patterns. It is not an empirical calibration study or evidence of improved learning. Small differences and profile-specific failures should guide revision, not promotional claims.
