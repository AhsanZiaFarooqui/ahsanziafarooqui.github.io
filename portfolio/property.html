Why this does not establish causal superiority

The two error-generating processes are assumed, not estimated from ground-truth observations. A fair validation study would require independent, property-level benchmarks, identical coverage, timing controls and tests for systematic error across relevant groups. Policy conclusions would also require correction mechanisms and revenue-authority capacity.
