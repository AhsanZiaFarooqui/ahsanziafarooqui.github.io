    What this portfolio proves, and what it does not

    It demonstrates authored evaluation cases, transparent reference solutions and explicit rubrics. It does not prove the author has been hired as an AI evaluator, nor establish cross-model benchmark reliability. A production benchmark needs independent expert annotation, blinded testing, dataset curation, contamination safeguards and agreement analysis.
