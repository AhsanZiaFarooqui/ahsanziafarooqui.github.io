Property Valuation Lab

Estimate the sale value of a comparable property using land area, covered floor area, and neighborhood. Explore a random forest model calibrated with Bayesian partial pooling. Manual image tracing and synthetic GeoJSON import demonstrate parcel and roof-footprint measurement.

Method and limitations. A browser-based random forest learns nonlinear patterns in observed sale prices. A separate Bayesian model shrinks neighborhood-specific out-of-sample residual adjustments toward zero. Predictions are demonstrative, not independent professional valuations. The example data are fictional; use verified transaction comparables for meaningful real-world analysis. No external model, API or server receives your uploaded rows.

What do these diagnostics establish?

The evaluation set was not used to fit either the forest or Bayesian adjustment. The interval is a plug-in Gaussian predictive approximation conditional on the trained forest, not a fully Bayesian credible interval or a conformal guarantee. Holdout coverage is reported precisely because the nominal 90% interval may be miscalibrated.

Train a model to reveal error metrics. Do not confuse training fit with predictive accuracy.

4. Satellite-image measurements for valuation

Upload an image you have permission to use (high-resolution satellite, orthophoto or drone image). Trace the land parcel and visible rooftop, and calibrate pixel distances using a known ground reference. Measurements can populate the land and covered-area valuation inputs above.

Not automatic satellite object detection. This interactive tool digitizes polygons you draw. A roof outline is not a total covered floor-area measurement; enter independently established floors and a justified adjustment factor. Without a trustworthy ground scale, pixel areas cannot be converted to square feet. The default image is an explicitly fictional overhead diagram.

Select reference segment, click two endpoints whose distance on the ground is independently known, then enter that distance in meters. Trace parcel and roof vertices and press Finish polygon for each. Do not infer scale from an unverified screenshot or stretch-distorted image.

Remote-sensing provenance and reliability

The image and annotations stay in the browser and are not sent to our server. This module does not estimate parcel ownership, market value, number of floors, or building condition from pixels. Imagery from different dates may not match the sale date or current building. Roof outlines can contain overhangs, shadows, and georegistration errors. For serious analysis, use georeferenced and licensed imagery, validated parcel boundaries, ground-truth floor measurements, blind holdout appraisal records, and spatially blocked validation.

Model specification and responsible interpretation

Random forest

48 seeded bootstrap regression trees predict log(sale_price_pkr) using land area, covered floor area and categorical neighborhood membership. Each split searches both continuous thresholds and neighborhood equality, without imposing a numeric ordering on neighborhoods.

Bayesian partial pooling

On a disjoint calibration sample, neighborhood residual means update Gaussian priors centered at zero. Small neighborhoods receive greater shrinkage. Predicted log value = forest log prediction + posterior residual adjustment.

What this model deliberately does not capture

Transaction date, exact location, legal title, road frontage, construction quality, condition, structural type, amenities, flooding risk, neighborhood boundaries, incentives, and selection into observed sales can all matter. In a changing market, mixing historical sales without controlling for dates is unsound. This is a research portfolio demonstrator, not a valuation service for buying, lending, or taxation.

How would this be validated in a research study?

Use geographically and temporally held-out transactions, assess subgroup performance, compare to hedonic regression and nonadaptive alternatives, evaluate interval coverage, and inspect errors for spatial dependence. Retrain only within a documented training protocol. A single random holdout may overstate performance when nearby or repeated properties appear in both samples.
