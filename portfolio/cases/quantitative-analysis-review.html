Econometrics · Data auditing

Case 02 · Quantitative analysis review

1. Client task

A fictional tax department compares ten circles adopting digital assessment in 2024 with ten comparison circles. The reviewer receives two years of synthetic circle-level tax collections and a deliberately corrupted export.

2. Hypothetical AI-generated deliverable

An illustrative AI readout calls a 20-million-PKR 2024 treated-control difference a statistically significant causal effect, does not inspect duplicate keys, and treats repeated circle-years as independent observations.

3. Expert issue register

ProblemWhy it mattersCorrection
Nonunique panel keyThe dirty CSV adds a second T03-2024 observation with value 999 and an extra blank C04-2024 observation.Assert unique (circle, year) and nonmissing tax collection before any summary.
Observed post difference mistaken for DiD110 - 90 = 20 million PKR is a post-only contrast.Use change in treated group minus change in comparison group: 15 million PKR.
Unjustified causalityDigitization assignment is not known to be random and only one preyear is available.Treat 15 as descriptive; investigate rollout assignment, earlier trends and other shocks.
Unverified significanceNo valid uncertainty interval or reported test supports a p-value.Prespecify estimator and dependence-aware inference; do not invent significance.
Incorrect effective sample unitTwo annual records per circle can share shocks.Account for repeated outcomes within circles and level of treatment assignment.
Unsupported rollout claimA single fictional comparison has no external validity.Assess other locations, data quality, implementation cost and contextual heterogeneity.

4. Independently verifiable evidence

MeasureVerified value
Digitized circles 2023 → 202480 → 110 million PKR; change +30
Comparison circles 2023 → 202475 → 90 million PKR; change +15
Post-treatment difference110 - 90 = 20 million PKR
Descriptive DiD(110 - 80) - (90 - 75) = 15 million PKR

5. Corrected response

The correct readout reports 15 million PKR as a descriptive difference-in-differences comparison. It cannot be called causal until the counterfactual trend is defended. A single preyear cannot establish parallel trends.

6. Reviewer verdict

Reject the hypothetical AI causal and significance statements. Correct the invalid panel, recompute group-year means, then report a design-limited comparison rather than an unconditional policy effect.
