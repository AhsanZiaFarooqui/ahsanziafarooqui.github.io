Case 03 · Professional deliverable review

1. Client task

A fictional agency seeks 2,500 completed household interviews, a defensible sampling plan and auditable QA, with a project funding ceiling of PKR 5.8 million.

2. Hypothetical AI-generated deliverable

An illustrative AI project plan recommends immediate approval despite a mismatched budget, a pilot before procurement and frame delivery, unrealistic enumerator capacity, an undefined 80% response-rate KPI and a related-party vendor recommendation.

3. Expert issue register

ProblemWhy it mattersCorrection
Budget does not add upStaffing 1.5m + fieldwork 2.2m + data 1.8m + QA/travel 0.9m = 6.4m, not 5.8m.Reconcile the 0.6m shortfall or obtain authorized funding before signing.
Dependencies reverse chronological orderPilot on December 10 comes before contracting December 15 and frame delivery December 20.Validate frame, contract, program instrument and pilot before main fieldwork.
Insufficient interview capacity20 enumerators × 4 completions/day × 20 days = 1,600, short by 900 interviews.Pilot production rates, revisit assumptions and rescope or add resources.
Undefined performance indicator80% response has no eligible-contact denominator or treatment of partial/unknown cases.Specify disposition taxonomy, eligibility and field-quality gates.
Conflict of interestSelection of a director's family firm without disclosure creates an integrity risk.Pause award, declare conflicts and use governed, independently reviewed criteria.

5. Corrected response

The plan must be held for revision. Budget and staffing must be costed together, the frame and procurement must precede the pilot, a realistic contingency is necessary, response denominators must be specified, and procurement rules and conflicts require independent review.

6. Reviewer verdict

Do not approve the illustrative AI-generated project plan. Numerical failures and a serious procurement integrity concern are decision-blocking defects that cannot be overridden by a polished narrative.
