Case 01 · Survey instrument review

1. Client task

A fictional municipality seeks descriptive estimates of adult residents' online-service use, satisfaction among users, and reasons for nonuse. A field-ready questionnaire must screen eligibility, use coherent skip logic, offer neutral responses and state analysis denominators.

2. Hypothetical AI-generated deliverable

An illustrative AI-authored survey asks everybody whether an excellent digital portal improved speed and trust, forces nonusers to report completion times and complaint outcomes, and claims an 80% response rate without a sample disposition table.

3. Expert issue register

ProblemWhy it mattersCorrection
Leading and double-barreled wordingIt presupposes the service is excellent and combines speed with trust.Separate constructs; ask a neutral satisfaction question and a distinct time item.
No eligibility or use-specific routingNonusers would be asked to invent experience with the portal.Screen adults resident in the municipality; only ask experience questions of confirmed users.
Complaint-status gate absentRespondents without a complaint receive an inapplicable question.Ask status only when the respondent confirms submitting a complaint.
Forced answers and sensitive valuesNo cannot-recall, prefer-not-to-say or not-applicable options.Add defensible missingness and privacy choices.
Causal overclaimObserved perceptions cannot identify the effect of digitization on trust.Report descriptive responses; identify causal claims with a separate evaluation design.
Unspecified response-rate denominatorNeither eligible nonrespondents nor unknown-eligibility contacts are accounted for.Report exact disposition and denominator, not a claimed 80%.

4. Independently verifiable evidence

MeasureVerified value
Eligible and completed96 of 150 known eligible, or 64.0%
Conservative denominator96 of (150 known eligible + 30 unknown), or 53.3%
Disposition200 contacts: 20 ineligible, 150 known eligible, 30 eligibility unknown
Important restrictionThese are completion proportions, not a standardized AAPOR response rate.

5. Corrected response

The revised nine-question instrument separates eligibility, use, experience, complaints and non-use. Routing is explicit (Q5 only if Q4=Yes; Q7 only if S1=No), and the wording does not ask respondents to infer a causal effect.

6. Reviewer verdict

Reject the hypothetical AI draft for field deployment. Its flawed construct definitions, skip logic and asserted response rate would bias interpretation. The replacement is only a proposed draft, pending piloting, translation and ethics review.
