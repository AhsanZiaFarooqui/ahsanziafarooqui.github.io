Research portfolio · Applied economics and quantitative evaluation

Evaluating evidence, experimental design and computational methods

I am an applied economist working on causal inference, research design, public finance and quantitative evaluation. This page presents selected methodological demonstrations and planned reproducible tools. Public demonstrations use synthetic data and do not disclose confidential research materials.

Prototype in development

AdaptiveChoice Lab

A synthetic Bayesian preference-learning demonstrator exploring adaptive choice questions, posterior updates and the difference between preference elicitation and validated causal claims. A standalone local prototype has been prepared; its methodological and accessibility review is pending before public deployment.
