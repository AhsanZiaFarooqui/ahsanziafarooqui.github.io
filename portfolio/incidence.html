Tax Incidence Simulation Lab

Calculate a synthetic property-tax schedule for 100 artificial households and explore statutory burdens by income quintile.

Critical distinction: Statutory liabilities are computed mechanically. The owner/tenant “burden allocation” is a user-supplied assumption, not an estimated equilibrium response. This is not a structural incidence model, HIES analysis, or calibrated Pakistan simulation.

“Quintile” is assigned by the synthetic generator, which imposes increasing income ranges. For genuine incidence analysis, quantiles must be constructed from observed or survey-weighted disposable income or consumption, and household/population weights must be documented.

Research-grade model requirements

A real economic-incidence model would distinguish owners, occupiers, landlords and renters, assign heterogeneous tax liability correctly, model capitalization and rent adjustment, incorporate behavioral responses and specify timing. It would require transparent calibration, identification, uncertainty analysis and data permissions. The current slider does none of those things.

Fictional distributions and statutory rules; no empirical estimates or official tax calculator claims.
